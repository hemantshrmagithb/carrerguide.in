# 🎓 CareerGuide.in

[![Deployed on Netlify](https://img.shields.io/badge/Deployed%20on-Netlify-00C7B7?logo=netlify)](https://verdant-jalebi-38821e.netlify.app/)

**CareerGuide.in** is a dedicated web platform designed to help students navigate the crucial question: *"Class 12 ke baad kya karun?"* (What to do after Class 12?). It provides clear, stream-wise career choices, breaking down both private professional courses and government job opportunities for Science, Commerce, and Arts students.

🌍 **Live Demo:** [https://verdant-jalebi-38821e.netlify.app/](https://verdant-jalebi-38821e.netlify.app/)

---

## ✨ Features

* **🛤️ Stream-Specific Guidance:** Curated roadmaps for Science (PCM/PCB), Commerce, and Arts/Humanities streams.
* **🏢 Private vs. Govt Paths:** Detailed breakdowns of professional degrees (B.Tech, CA, BA LLB, etc.) alongside immediate and post-graduation government job tracks (NDA, SSC, Banking, Civil Services).
* **💡 Smart Career Tips:** Actionable advice on internships, mentorship, focusing on strengths, and building backup plans.
* **❓ Interactive FAQs:** Answers to the most common questions students face regarding exams, graduation benefits, and alternate paths.
* **🗣️ Student Reviews:** Real feedback and testimonials from students who found clarity through the platform.
* **📱 Responsive UI:** Clean, mobile-friendly design ensuring students can access guidance easily from any device.
